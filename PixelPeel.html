INDUSTRY CHALLENGE

Quality Assessment Shouldn't Depend on Guesswork

Traditional inspection at agricultural mandis and distribution centers relies on manual visual checks that lead to quality variance, unfair settlements, and procurement slowdowns.

Manual Inspection

Grading hundreds of bags manually causes severe physical fatigue, delays truck turnaround, and leads to superficial sample bias.

Human Subjectivity

Different inspectors evaluate surface blemishes differently. What one appraiser accepts as Grade A, another demotes to Grade B.

Procurement Disputes

Subjective quality disagreements result in payment rejections, arbitrations, and compromised trust between farmers and wholesale buyers.

Inconsistent Grading

Paper-based reports provide no visual audit trail. Retrospective audits are virtually impossible once produce leaves the loading dock.

SYSTEM PROTOCOL

5 Steps From Intake to Actionable Grade

Designed for seamless deployment directly in sorting docks, mandis, and packing warehouses.

01

Capture

Photograph individual samples or conveyer batches using a mobile phone, tablet, or industrial fixed overhead RGB camera.

02

Preprocess

PixelPeel corrects illumination variations, normalizes contrast, removes shadows, and segments the onion boundary.

03

Detect

Deep-learning bounding boxes localize surface abrasions, black mold, neck rot, root plate emergence, and sprout stems.

04

Grade

An algorithmic decision tree matches detected blemish ratios against national grading standards (Grade A, B, or C reject).

05

Report

An immutable digital quality assessment report is generated, complete with bounding snapshots and verifiable telemetry.

ALGORITHMIC FOUNDATION

Computer Vision at the Core

Built upon modern single-stage convolutional object detection architectures for real-time edge processing and low-latency inference.

YOLO Pipeline

Object Localization

Identifies onion contours, caliber dimensions, and surface boundaries against noisy background environments like conveyor belts, gunny sacks, or field soil.

Multi-Class CV

Defect Segmentation

Classifies visible pathology: distinguishing harmless dry-scale peeling from critical fungal rots, cuts, and commercial sprouting.

Image DSP

Image Processing

Dynamic histogram equalization and chromatic normalization mitigate uneven daylighting and dust glare common in rural procurement hubs.

Deterministic Engine

Quality Grading Rulebase

Converts defect pixel area calculations and surface confidence scores into actionable Grade A, B, and Commercial Reject ratings.
